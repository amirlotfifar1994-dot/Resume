THINK-360+ Interactive Platform

A Progressive Web Application (PWA) designed as an interactive companion to “Islands of Your Mind: 9 Thinking Styles for Everyday Life”. It translates cognitive-science inspired concepts into short guided exercises and structured reflections for everyday decision-making.

Overview

THINK-360+ organizes content around nine thinking styles and provides guided prompts, micro-exercises, and reflection tools to support deliberate strategy selection across contexts.

Nine thinking styles

🎯 Analytical ThinkingBreak down problems, reason logically, decide with evidence.
🌊 Holistic ThinkingSee systems, patterns, and interconnections.
💡 Creative ThinkingGenerate novel options and non-obvious links.
🔄 Flexible ThinkingShift strategies when constraints or context change.
🤝 Social ThinkingFactor in people, incentives, and relationships.
🧘 Reflective ThinkingSlow down, notice assumptions, and regulate impulse.
⚡ Action ThinkingExecute, iterate, learn by doing.
🧭 Ethical ThinkingConsider values, fairness, and downstream impact.
🔍 Critical ThinkingChallenge claims, detect bias, test alternatives.
