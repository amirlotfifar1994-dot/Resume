Part-time / project-based

Statistical consultant and data analyst

PVD Kavian Co. · Tehran

My work with Kavian has covered data preparation and reporting as well as statistical analysis: two-way ANOVA, factor analysis, and structural equation modeling with PLS-SEM. I turn the results into tables, reports, and dashboards that people can discuss and use in decisions.

Details
  • Data preparation and cleaning, checking missing values and analysis assumptions, and descriptive statistics.
  • Two-way ANOVA to examine the main effects of two factors and their interaction.
  • Exploratory and confirmatory factor analysis (EFA / CFA) to examine factor structure and measurement models.
  • Partial least squares structural equation modeling (PLS-SEM): measurement models, reliability and validity, and relationships between variables in the structural model.
  • Interpreting and reporting statistical results, preparing tables and charts, and building indicators and dashboards around operational questions.
