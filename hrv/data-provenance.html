  • figures/manipulation_check.png, figures/comprehensive_results.png – watermarked simulated figures.

Hypothesis Labelling Notes (v5.2 corrections)

H4 Label Clarification: The dashboard H4 panel displays the correlation between ΔlogRMSSD and EV_rel (r = 0.40, p < 0.001). This is technically the path-b association from the H2 mediation model — not the pre-registered H4 hypothesis, which specifies individual-difference moderators (GAD-7, BDI-II, PSQI × EV_rel). In the TESTMODE simulated data, questionnaire-performance correlations are not significant (GAD-7 × EV_rel: r = −0.03; BDI-II × EV_rel: r = 0.08; both n.s.), as expected for synthetic data without real clinical variance. The pre-registered H4 will be properly tested in the real-data phase.
H3 RL Parameters — Beta Capping: The RL parameters in this dashboard were estimated using maximum-likelihood (ML) point estimation with β bounded to [0, 5]. In the simulated data, 68.1% of participants (n = 109) hit the upper bound (β = 5.0), indicating that the true inverse-temperature is likely higher than the bound allows. This boundary artefact means H3 group comparisons for β should be interpreted with caution. The full hierarchical Bayesian estimation using dual_alpha_rl.stan (Section 9 of the repository) is planned for the real-data phase and will resolve this.

What This Bundle Is Not

  • Not a de-identified export of human participant data.
  • Not final RCT results.
  • Not sufficient by itself for a results-based preprint.

Requirements for a Results-Based Preprint

When real data are collected, the preprint will require: ethics approval details, CONSORT recruitment flow, data collection dates and location, primary and secondary outcomes computed from real trial data, a data availability statement or restricted-access policy, and reproducible scripts with checksums of raw inputs.
