01

Your Mind Islands (جزیره‌های ذهن تو)

Persian-language book · nine thinking lenses · ready for publication and print

Your Mind Islands looks at nine ways of approaching a problem. Examples and exercises let readers try each way of thinking in everyday decisions and relationships.

Companion apps ↓
Details & stack
  • About 62,000 words: introduction, a hands-on Chapter Zero, the Mind Workshop, nine “island” chapters, a closing chapter, and an afterword.
  • Appendices with a quick guide to cognitive traps and biases and a short glossary, plus an integrated reference list.
  • About 50 illustrations and a professional Word layout, iterated through numbered quality stages (visual polish, RTL lock, content updates).
  • Companion digital products: the THINK-360+ and Mind Islands web apps.

02

See the Child in Play (کودک را در بازی ببین)

Persian-language guide for parents · children aged 2–6 · currently being written

I am writing this guide for parents and educators of children aged 2–6. Play and observation lead into the discussion, with research findings alongside ideas to try in everyday interactions.

Details & stack
  • Integrated edition of about 77,000 words: 8 parts, 27 chapters, and 52 main games.
  • Every chapter follows the same structure: central question, what science says, notes for parents and for educators, a small adult experiment, and selected sources.
  • Chapter-by-chapter production workflow with frozen versions and a five-part review report for each chapter.
  • Careful wording about milestones and concerns: strengths first, and clear limits on what observation can and cannot tell.

03

The Half-Life of Meaning — نیمه‌عمر معنا

Human life, meaning, and artificial intelligence · work in progress

In The Half-Life of Meaning, I ask what happens to our role and sense of direction when AI takes on part of our work and learning. The book begins with familiar situations: work, relationships, belief, hope, and the experience of understanding.

Manuscript in developmentRead the book and research materials (Persian) ↗In the academic CV
About this work
  • The current manuscript has 16 chapters in six parts. It is available to read online and is still being revised.
  • Two accompanying conceptual papers examine purpose in the context of AI and the attribution of understanding. Review reports document how the arguments have changed.
  • The book and papers are works in progress, not peer-reviewed publications or empirical evidence for a historical law of meaning.

Full version: about 300 lessons · public demo: 6 sample lessons · English interface

Learning starts with a picture: learners explore scene details, discover words and phrases, and describe what they see. The full version contains about 300 lessons. The public SceneSpeak demo presents this visual learning journey through 6 sample lessons in the browser.

04

AmarAmooz — Interactive Descriptive Statistics

Persian / RTL · React + Vite · GitHub Pages

I built AmarAmooz for learning descriptive statistics in psychology. It includes 10 lessons, 480 exercises with worked answers, and 32 teaching documents. Interactive charts show what changes when the data changes.

Ordinal data lab ↗Live site ↗Source (GitHub)
Details & stack
  • Labs for nominal, ordinal and quantitative data, with charts and calculations matched to the measurement scale; ordinal categories do not assume equal intervals.
  • Interactive histogram, box plot, z-score and scatter plot labs: edit data and see the charts and calculations update together.
  • Persian numeral input, exercise search and filters, self-assessment, and progress and answer drafts saved in the same browser.
  • Downloadable Word handouts and the complete course archive, with a responsive interface for desktop and mobile.

05

Clinical Psychology Learning Assistant

Persian / RTL · Next.js + Prisma · development version, private repository

I built this platform for studying clinical psychology and practising reasoning. It brings together a disorder encyclopedia, libraries of therapy approaches and methods, and an assessment and differential-reasoning workshop.

Details & stack
  • Searchable disorder entries with symptoms, pre-diagnostic considerations, differentials and sources; therapy libraries with original examples and worksheets.
  • Differential-reasoning practice with fictional cases and guided questions; changing answers shows their effect on conditions and evidence.
  • Content management and review, responsive Persian interface, TypeScript and PostgreSQL through Prisma.
  • Educational version under development and scientific review; workshop outputs are not diagnostic probabilities or a clinical diagnostic tool.
