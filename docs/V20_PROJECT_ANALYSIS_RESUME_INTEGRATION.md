## Integrated project summaries

### Gold Shop Accounting PWA — v5.86.0 / P200
Positioned as a Persian/RTL local-first PWA/Electron accounting and management system for gold shops, with daily pricing, wage profiles, inventory controls, barcode/label flows, reconciliation/approval, executive reporting, and print/A4 hardening.

### Kavico Payroll Platform — v1.0.0-rc.1
Positioned as a local HR, attendance, payroll, payslip and BI platform with Node.js/SQLite backend, Electron desktop runtime, reporting/delivery engine, PDF/HTML handoff, safe email dry-run, audit and permission controls.

### Assessment MVP — v0.37.0 / Stage 9226
Positioned as a full-stack recruitment assessment and skill-testing platform with job-skill matrix, assessment builder, question bank, candidate runner, invites, practical review, reports, scoring, RBAC, Prisma and TypeScript architecture.

### Kavian PVD Management System — v5.11.0
Positioned as a self-contained offline management system for Kavian/PVD operations, with orders, customers, inventory, finance reports, saved views, dashboard pinned cards, deep links, backup/import/restore and audit.
